Northern Culture

HANOI - THANG LONG HERITAGE

The thousands-year-old capital reflecting Vietnam's ancient traditions and folk arts

Famous Landmarks & Historic Sites

Hoan Kiem Lake & Turtle Tower

The historic heart of Hanoi, famous for its serene waters, ancient Turtle Tower, and legendary myth of the Returned Sword.

Temple of Literature

Vietnam's first national university constructed in 1070, dedicated to Confucius and honoring ancient scholars.

Hanoi Old Quarter

A lively network of 36 historic commercial streets preserving centuries of artisanal craftsmanship and local life.

One Pillar Pagoda

An architectural marvel built in 1049, designed to resemble a delicate lotus blossom emerging from the water.

Traditional Water Puppetry Art

Water Puppetry (Múa Rối Nước) - A unique intangible folk art originated in the Red River Delta during the 11th century.

Iconic Northern Cuisine

Hanoi Pho (Beef Noodle Soup)

World-renowned traditional soup featuring flat rice noodles, fragrant herb-infused clear beef broth, and tender sliced beef.

Bun Cha Hanoi

Charcoal-grilled pork patties and pork belly served in sweet dipping sauce, accompanied by rice vermicelli and fresh herbs.

Hanoi Egg Coffee (Cà Phê Trứng)

A rich specialty prepared with dark Vietnamese coffee topped with whipped egg yolks and sweetened condensed milk.

Central Culture

HUE IMPERIAL CITY - ROYAL ELEGANCE

The ancient imperial capital preserving royal monuments and UNESCO world heritages

Imperial Monuments of Hue

Imperial Citadel of Hue

A UNESCO World Heritage site, serving as the seat of the Nguyen Dynasty emperors for over 140 years (1802-1945).

Thien Mu Pagoda

An iconic 7-story octagonal tower built in 1601, standing peacefully along the scenic Perfume River.

Tomb of Emperor Khai Dinh

An architectural masterpiece combining traditional Vietnamese mosaic art with European Gothic influences.

Hue Royal Court Music (Nha Nhac)

Nha Nhac Cung Dinh Hue - Recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

Royal & Authentic Central Dishes

Bun Bo Hue (Spicy Beef Noodles)

A rich, aromatic noodle soup featuring lemongrass broth, tender beef shank, crab balls, and spicy chili oil.

Hue Steamed Rice Cakes (Banh Beo)

Delicate small rice flour cakes topped with dried shrimp, crispy pork skin, scallions, and savory fish sauce.

Clam Rice (Cơm Hến)

A vibrant harmony of rice, baby river clams, crispy pork cracklings, roasted peanuts, and fragrant herbs.

Southern Culture

HO CHI MINH CITY - SAIGON DYNAMISM

A vibrant metropolis blending modern energy, colonial charm, and hospitable traditions

Saigon Iconic Landmarks

Saigon Notre-Dame Basilica

An iconic French colonial cathedral constructed in the 19th century with red bricks imported directly from Marseille.

Ben Thanh Market

The historic bustling heart of Saigon commerce, offering traditional handicrafts, textiles, and local street delicacies.

Independence Palace

A historic national landmark preserving 1970s architecture, deeply associated with the modern history of Vietnam.

Don Ca Tai Tu Music Heritage

Don Ca Tai Tu - Soulful southern folk musical art reflecting the warm hospitality of the Mekong Delta.

Flavorful Southern Street Food

Saigon Banh Mi

Crispy French baguette stuffed with savory pate, cold cuts, fresh cilantro, cucumber, pickled daikon, and chili.

Saigon Broken Rice (Cơm Tấm)

Popular dish served with marinated grilled pork chop, steamed egg loaf, pork skin, and sweet garlic fish sauce.

Hu Tieu Noodle Soup

A flavorful noodle dish featuring rich pork bone broth, fresh shrimp, quail eggs, minced pork, and fresh herbs.
